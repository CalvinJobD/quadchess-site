What is Quad Chess?

Quad Chess is chess with four boards in play at once. A regular 8x8 chess board sits in the center, surrounded by three triangle worlds:

  • Rook's World (5x5): two full rows of rooks for each side.
  • Pawn World (5x5): two full rows of pawns for each side.
  • Bishop World (7x7): two rows of alternating bishops and knights for each side.

Fight your way to the Morph Tunnel squares on your opponent's back row of a triangle world, and your piece slides down the tunnel onto the center board's landing row. Tunnels are one-way, so every trip counts.

It's a two-player game on one iPhone: pass the phone back and forth. Player 1 plays Ivory and Player 2 plays Crimson. Checkmate the king, or capture it, on the center board to win.

  • Spin the three worlds around the center board with your finger.
  • Built-in How to Play and "How the Pieces Move" guides for new players.
  • For all ages. No ads, no accounts, no in-app purchases, and it works offline.

Quick rules

  • The reply rule: after your opponent moves on a triangle board, you get 2 moves on the center board, then you must answer on a triangle board.
  • Morph Tunnels: reaching a tunnel square on the opponent's back row of a triangle world sends your piece down to the center board (row 3 for Player 1, row 6 for Player 2).
  • Winning: checkmate, or capture the king, on the center board.
